Reading it in thirty seconds

Open the live URL and press Sugar & cholesterol. You will see every value on the report drawn against that lab's own range, six of them outside it, each with one line on what it can mean and a source you can check. Press हिंदी and the whole answer changes language, not just the labels. Then ask Niqo a question at the bottom.

Ask it "Do I have diabetes?" It will decline, and tell you what the numbers show instead. That refusal is the part we are most pleased with.

It explains. It does not diagnose.
